AMT Mining Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AMT Mining ("the App") handles information when you use the app.

1. Information We Process

When you sign in with Pi, the App may receive information made available by the Pi Platform, such as your Pi user identifier and Pi username. The App may also process information that you voluntarily provide, such as a wallet address, referral information, profile information, and app activity needed to operate the service.

2. Mining and Account Data

The App may store account-related records such as mining sessions, mining activity, AMT ledger entries, referrals, reputation information, and wallet connection status. These records are used to provide the App's features and maintain account continuity.

3. Payments

If payment features are enabled, payment information and payment transaction identifiers may be processed through the Pi Platform and the App's backend to verify and complete transactions. The App does not request or store your Pi wallet private key or passphrase.

4. How Information Is Used

  • To authenticate your Pi account.
  • To provide mining, wallet, referral, profile, and related features.
  • To maintain records associated with your account.
  • To prevent duplicate or unauthorized account activity.
  • To process and verify supported transactions.
  • To maintain and improve the App.

5. Information Sharing

The App does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the App, including hosting, database, and Pi Platform services, or when disclosure is required by law.

6. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored account information. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

Account and transaction-related records may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain accounting and security records, resolve disputes, or comply with applicable requirements.

8. Your Choices

You may stop using the App at any time. Where applicable, you may request information about your stored account data or request deletion, subject to records that must be retained for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact the App operator.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features or requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions concerning AMT Mining, contact the App operator through the contact method provided with the AMT Mining application.
